CASE STUDIES

We don't just build software. We solve business problems.

RETAIL · INVENTORY
PROBLEM

The business managed 2,000+ products through spreadsheets, with no visibility into real stock levels.

SOLUTION

A centralized inventory system with barcode scanning, low-stock alerts and multi-location tracking.

RESULT

Real-time stock visibility, automated reporting and faster order processing across every outlet.

DISTRIBUTION · SALES
PROBLEM

SOLUTION

A mobile sales app connected to a live CRM and billing dashboard for the whole team.

RESULT

Order-to-invoice time cut significantly, with full visibility into every sales rep's pipeline.
